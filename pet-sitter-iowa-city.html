Pet sitter — Iowa City: caring, local, dependable

If you search for "pet sitter Iowa City," you want someone who truly understands local neighborhoods and day-to-day routines. Hawkeye Cozy Paws is run by long-time residents who know how to match schedules, accommodate busy students and local professionals, and keep your home secure while you are away.

Meet our core sitters

Emma Turner — Lead sitter & client coordinator

Emma grew up on a small family farm outside Iowa City and has 10+ years of dog-handling experience. She manages bookings and trains new sitters to follow owner routines and medication schedules.

Luis Chen — Overnight specialist & behavior observer

Luis studied animal behavior and focuses on anxious pets and multi-pet households. He emphasizes slow introductions and calm, reassuring handling.

Asha Malik — Cat care & small pets

Asha specializes in cats, rabbits, and other small pets. She is meticulous about litter routines, enrichment, and safe handling for timid animals.

Seasonal sitters

We hire carefully screened seasonal sitters for peak times such as holidays and University breaks, and every sitter completes a checklist-based onboarding with a senior team member.

Services for Iowa City residents

We offer 30- and 60-minute visits, daily dog walks, med administration, plant checks, mail pickup, and overnight in-home stays. All services include at least one photo update and a short visit note.

Safety and reliability

Background checks, local references, and liability coverage are standard. We use a secure key-management system for each household and provide emergency vet transport or notification if needed (with prior authorization).
