Overnight pet care in Iowa City

Overnight stays allow your pet to remain in the comfort of home with a dedicated sitter on-site. For many pets —especially anxious dogs or cats with strict routines—an overnight sitter reduces stress and maintains normal habits.

What an overnight service includes

  • Evening arrival, overnight presence, and morning check-out
  • Meal and medication administration as instructed
  • Walks or litter box maintenance on the sitter's schedule
  • At least two photo updates and a morning summary
  • Emergency contact with vet or owner and transport if pre-authorized

When overnight care is a good choice

Choose overnight in-home care when your pet does best in familiar surroundings, when separation anxiety is a concern, or when you need a sitter to maintain exact medication timing. Overnight stays are also ideal for multi-pet homes where social dynamics are delicate.

Pricing and availability

Our overnight service is a flat fee within Iowa City limits. Pricing varies by holiday dates and additional pets. Contact us for a personalized quote and to check sitter availability for University breaks and holidays.

Common questions

How do you handle medications?

We document dosing times and keep medication in a secure, labeled container. Sitters record each dose in the visit note and confirm by photo when appropriate.

Are sitters trained for medical emergencies?

Sitters are not substitutes for veterinary care, but they are trained to recognize urgent symptoms and will contact owners and an emergency clinic immediately per your instructions.

Can I request the same sitter every time?

We do our best to assign the same sitter for repeat bookings. We recommend booking early to reserve preferred sitters for holidays.
