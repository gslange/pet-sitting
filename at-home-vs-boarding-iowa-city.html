At-home pet sitting vs boarding — which is right for your pet?

Choosing between at-home pet sitting and boarding depends on temperament, health needs, and what helps your pet stay calm and happy while you're away. Below we compare common factors to help you decide.

At-home pet sitting (in your home)

  • Familiar environment: Pets stay in their own beds and follow their usual routines.
  • Lower stress for many pets: No transport or new noises; especially beneficial for cats and senior dogs.
  • Personalized attention: One-on-one care and communication tailored to your instructions.
  • Home security: Mail, lights, and thermostat changes can be managed as part of the service.

When to choose at-home sitting: Your pet has medication needs, separation anxiety, or simply does best at home.

Boarding (kennel or pet hotel)

  • Supervised group environment: Good for highly social dogs who enjoy playgroups and structured exercise.
  • On-site staff: 24/7 facility staff may be available in many boarding facilities, providing consistent supervision.
  • Activity and socialization: Daily playtimes and interaction with other dogs under staff guidance.
  • Medical handling varies: Some boarding facilities can handle medications, but confirm qualifications ahead of time.

When to choose boarding: Your pet loves social interaction, or you prefer a facility with round-the-clock staff and on-site back-up for acute incidents.

Practical tips for Iowa City owners

- Meet the sitter in your home before booking to confirm they follow your routines.
- If boarding, tour the facility and ask about staffing levels, cleaning protocols, and vaccination requirements.
- For seasonal breaks in Iowa City (University move-outs, holidays), reserve early—both sitters and reputable kennels book quickly.
